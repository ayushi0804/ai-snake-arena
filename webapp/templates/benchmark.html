AI Benchmarking Lab

What is measured

Pathfinding benchmarks run A*, BFS and Dijkstra on identical seeded board states and measure time (min of 3 repeats), nodes expanded, path length, success rate, and whether the path is safe (a virtual snake can follow it and still reach its tail). Survival benchmarks play whole seeded games and are reported separately, because a shortest path says nothing about surviving afterwards.

On this unweighted grid BFS, Dijkstra and A* (Manhattan heuristic, admissible) all find shortest paths; they differ in effort. With weighted terrain only Dijkstra/A* stay optimal, and BFS does not. Timings come from the server's Python engine and vary by machine.
